Third-party notices for betterdraw

betterdraw copies some fonts, icons and code from other projects into its own source tree. These notices credit them: each entry gives its copyright lines and names its license, and the full texts of those licenses come at the end, each given once. A betterdraw server serves these notices at /licenses.txt, followed by the licenses of the npm packages it uses.
